Project interests

Possible directions to explore:

Same question, different politics?

Ask AI models the same policy questions with different political framing. Compare which arguments and sources they emphasize, and build an interactive guide to the differences.

Can a label change what we believe?

Design a study using fictional campaign messages to explore whether AI-generated content labels change perceived credibility. Connect the findings to political advertising disclosure.

Where does government use AI?

Build a small dashboard from public agency disclosures showing what AI systems do, what people are told, and whether decisions can be appealed. Compare the transparency gaps.
